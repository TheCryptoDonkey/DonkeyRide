🫏 How DonkeyRide works

DonkeyRide connects riders and drivers without a company in the middle. There is no sign-up, no commission taken from your fare, and payment goes directly from rider to driver — cash, Lightning, M-Pesa or ecash. The operator only coordinates: it never holds your money and cannot read your messages.

No account needed

The first time you open the app it creates an Account ID and a recovery key on your phone. That key is your account: back it up from the profile page, and you can restore your identity — and your reputation — on any phone, with any operator. Nobody can reset it for you, because nobody else has it.

For riders

Requesting

  • Your pickup is already set to where you are — named by street, not just a pin — so the only thing to answer is "Where to?". Change it any time by searching, tapping the map, or dragging the pin.
  • Add up to 3 stops along the way — the fare covers the full route, shown up front in your local currency. Pin Home and Work in the search for one-tap reuse.
  • Women-only: if you've declared you're a woman on your profile, you can ask to be matched only with drivers who have declared the same. It's self-declared — there are no accounts to verify against — so always check the car, registration and pickup code before getting in.
  • Ride now, or book up to 30 days ahead — a driver can commit early, and you both get a reminder near the time.
  • Driver ratings are cryptographically signed and verified on your own phone — not numbers the operator asserts.

Meeting your driver

  • You see how many minutes away your driver is, live, while they come to you.
  • Moved? Move your pickup. People walk — out of the pub, round to a legal kerb. Tap "I'm here now", search a new spot, or drag the pin, right up until your driver arrives. Your driver is told immediately, and the agreed fare does not change (short moves only, once a driver has committed).
  • At match you see the driver's rating and their car — colour, make, model and registration.
  • Both phones derive the same pickup code (a PIN and a word) from your encrypted connection. Only your real driver's phone can show it — an impostor car cannot.
  • Chat is end-to-end encrypted. The operator cannot read a word of it.

Staying safe

  • Share your trip with people you trust: they get an encrypted note (driver, car, route area) straight to their Nostr messages — any NIP-17 messaging app works, no DonkeyRide needed. Arrival sends them an automatic all-clear. Mark a contact "every trip" and sharing happens by itself.
  • Ride check: your own phone watches the trip. If it leaves the expected route or stops for too long, you're asked "Everything OK?" — and if you don't answer within a minute, the people you shared with are alerted automatically.
  • The panic button alerts your trusted contacts with your last known location and offers a one-tap call to local emergency services.
  • Record audio during a trip if it makes you feel safer: it stays on your phone only, encrypted to your account key, and deletes itself after 72 hours unless you export it. The other person is told in chat, and recording-consent laws vary by place — complying with yours is your responsibility.
  • If your driver never shows up, tick "didn't show up" when you cancel: a report signed by you attaches to their public reputation, so future riders see it. The same applies to riders who leave drivers waiting — with no company in the middle, accountability is reputation, not fees.

Paying

  • Cash — hand it over, done.
  • Lightning — pay the driver's invoice from any wallet, or connect your own wallet in the app.
  • M-Pesa — Send Money direct to the driver's number (or over Lightning via Tando).
  • Cashu ecash — send the token in the encrypted chat; only your driver can redeem it.

Whatever the method, the money moves from you to the driver. The operator transmits nothing.

Your trip history lives on your phone only — the operator keeps no record of where you've been.

For drivers

  • Women drivers can declare it on the profile page to receive women-only requests, and optionally choose to receive only those.
  • You keep the whole fare, and it settles instantly — no weekly payout, no cashout fee. Add your payment methods (Lightning Address, M-Pesa number, Cashu, cash) on the profile page.
  • Go online to receive nearby jobs live, and browse every waiting request — including pre-booked rides you can commit to early, and jobs from other operators over Nostr.
  • Draw your own working areas on the map, or set a destination to only see jobs heading your way. You choose your work — nothing is centrally assigned.
  • Every job shows the fare, distance and rider reputation before you accept. Exact locations are revealed only after.
  • Set your vehicle once so riders can spot your car.
  • A rider can move their pickup a short distance while you're on your way (they walk). You're told the moment it happens and your map updates — the fare you agreed stays the same, and it's frozen once you arrive.
  • On Android, install the driver app — jobs keep arriving with the screen off.

What the operator can and cannot see

Cannot: read your chat, see who you shared a trip with, derive your pickup code, hear your trip recordings, touch your money, or keep your trip history.

Can: coordinate the ride while it's live — exact locations are held in memory, shared only between the matched rider and driver, and forgotten. Public records carry approximate areas only.

Language

DonkeyRide speaks English and Kiswahili — it follows your phone's language automatically, and you can switch on the profile page.

Questions

DonkeyRide is free, open-source software implementing the TROTT protocol — anyone can run an operator, and your account travels with you.
